📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze employee data and uncover key insights related to attrition, demographics, salary trends, and job roles.
This project helps HR teams make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

🚀 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides a visual and analytical view of employee attrition patterns across different parameters such as age, gender, education, salary, experience, and job roles.
It enables organizations to identify risk areas and take proactive HR actions.

🎯 Objectives

• Analyze employee attrition trends

• Identify key factors influencing attrition

• Support strategic HR decision-making

• Improve employee retention and satisfaction

📈 Dashboard Insights

🔹 Attrition by Gender

Male employees show higher attrition compared to females.

🔹 Attrition by Age Group

Highest attrition observed in 26–35 and 36–45 age groups.

🔹 Attrition by Education

Medical and Life Sciences backgrounds contribute to the major share of attrition.

Technical Degree holders show minimal attrition.

🔹 Attrition by Salary

Employees earning below 5K have the highest attrition rate.

Attrition decreases as salary increases.

🔹 Attrition by Experience

Employees with early and mid-level experience show higher attrition.

Long-tenure employees tend to stay longer.

🔹 Attrition by Job Role

Highest attrition observed in:

• Research Scientist

• Laboratory Technician

• Sales Executive
